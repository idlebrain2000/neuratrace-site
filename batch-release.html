What NeuraTrace does

"Show me everything I need to know about Batch FP-2411-A."

Evidence reconstructed

Every record across every connected system that bears on this batch, assembled through the graph rather than by hand.

Required records checked

Deterministic checks against your own SOPs and STPs, each resolving to expected, actual and the system the value came from.

Exceptions surfaced

What deserves attention and why, rather than a flat dump of everything the systems hold.

Provenance attached

Every value names its source system and record, persisted for the full retention period.

Observations captured

The reviewer must open every flagged item and record what they found before the section closes.

Approver stays independent

Reviewer and approver are separate roles with separate records. Neither can authorise while a flag is unaddressed.
