Life sciences data is connected in reality. Your systems aren't.

A product exists across dozens of systems. Its materials live in SAP. Manufacturing history in MES. Test results in LIMS and chromatography systems. Deviations and CAPAs in QMS. Regulatory context in RIMS. Procedures and specifications in documents.

Each system records part of the truth

None of them models the complete context

No system owns the relationships between them — which material lot went into which batch, which specification governed which test, which deviation touched what.

Architecture

Keep your systems of record. Connect the context between them.

NeuraTrace does not replace SAP, LIMS, MES, QMS or RIMS. It sits above them as a read-only context and evidence layer.

01

No rip-and-replace

Your systems keep doing exactly what they do today. Nothing is migrated, nothing is decommissioned, and no team has to change how they work to get value.

02

No new transactional system

NeuraTrace is not another place to enter data. It reads what your systems already hold and models how those records relate.

03

No write-back required

Every connector is independently read-only. No shared write path exists anywhere in the architecture. Your systems remain the systems of record.

How we use AI

AI reconstructs context. Humans remain accountable.

In regulated life sciences, restraint is a feature. Every constraint below is architectural — not a setting we offer reluctantly.

PRINCIPLE 01

Evidence over inference

Every material finding links back to source evidence. Nothing is asserted that an expert cannot open and read for themselves.

PRINCIPLE 02

Deterministic where the requirement is

A specification limit is a comparison, not a judgment call. Whether a standard was expired is a lookup, not an opinion. AI is used where it cannot make a compliance decision.

PRINCIPLE 03

Human-controlled decisions

NeuraTrace does not release a batch. It does not close a deviation. It does not recommend release. It assembles what the expert needs and gets out of the way.

PRINCIPLE 04

Every answer is traceable

AI-generated conclusions stay connected to the source records they were derived from. An answer that cannot be followed back to its evidence is not an answer we will ship.

What we deliberately do not track

NeuraTrace does not record individual reviewer activity time. Our design partner told us directly that regulators weaponise that data in inspections. We also removed analyst qualification from the instruments view, because surfacing it draws auditor attention to qualification records that were never in scope.

Your vocabulary, not ours

Never "approved" — always review authorised. Never "passed" — always compliant. The workspace speaks the language of your SOPs, because a term that does not appear in your quality system is a term someone has to defend.

Evidence & provenance

Context without provenance is just another AI answer.

Every finding names its source. A reviewer can follow any conclusion back to the record it came from, in the system it came from — and still do it in seven years, when the person who signed it has left and an inspector asks.

Two-level workflow

Reviewer and approver are separate roles with separate records. Neither can authorise a section while a flag is unaddressed.

Observation is mandatory

The reviewer must open every flagged item and record what they found before the section closes. It is a gate, not a suggestion.

Provenance on every value

Each figure names the system it was retrieved from, so reconstruction never depends on anyone's memory.
