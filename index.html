The problem

Your batch is connected. Your evidence isn't.

Quality evidence lives across ERP, LIMS, chromatography systems, MES, QMS and documents. Reviewers still have to find it, reconcile it and interpret it by hand — every batch, every time.

01

Chasing records

Hours spent locating the evidence behind one decision, across five or more systems and a filing cabinet.

02

Reconciling data

Manual cross-checks between records, results and specifications, with no shared view of what belongs together.

03

Managing backlogs

Limited visibility into what is blocked, why it is blocked, and which batches actually need a reviewer's attention.

04

Preparing for audits

Reconstructing the trail months or years later, when the reviewer who signed it has moved on.

What that costs you

The reviewer becomes the integration layer.

What changes

From scattered records to a review you can explain.

01

Missing evidence, inconsistent values and the quality events that bear on this batch, surfaced before the reviewer opens it.

Two-level workflow

Reviewer and approver are separate roles with separate records. Neither can authorise a section while a flag is unaddressed.

Observation is mandatory

Every flagged item must be opened and answered in writing before the section closes. It is a gate, not a suggestion.

Provenance on every value

Each figure names the system it was retrieved from, so reconstruction never depends on anyone's memory.

Platform & trust

Works with your systems. Keeps people accountable.

NeuraTrace does not replace your LIMS, QMS, MES or ERP, and never becomes a system of record. It reads what they already hold.

READ-ONLY

No write path

Every connector is independently read-only. No component holds write credentials to any system of record.

DETERMINISTIC

Rules, not opinions

IN YOUR ENVIRONMENT

Deployed inside your control boundary

On-premises inside the plant network, with role-based access, 21 CFR Part 11 e-signatures and a GAMP 5 validation pack.

How it works underneath. NeuraTrace builds a connected model of the entities a quality decision depends on — batches, materials, lots, tests, specifications, equipment, deviations, CAPAs and the documents behind them — and keeps every value's source, version and timestamp attached. That model is what makes one connected review possible, and what lets the same evidence serve deviation investigation, CAPA and inspection response later.
